The same model, drawn instead of written. Lightness is still
       L = 100 − 100·t^e with e = base(t) − 0.20·w(H)·b(t) —
       only now base, b and w are Bézier chains
       you drag — round anchors, square handles. Two anchors and two handles for
       each Hill; three anchors for the bell and for each hue lobe, the middle one
       with a handle on either side. The handles open fitted to the v7 functions,
       as close as that many anchors reach: the cos² lobes land on them almost
       exactly, the Hills turn twice against one segment and carry the rest.
       The x-axis on each t curve is logarithmic: the tokens are a
       doubling scale, so they space out evenly and the light tints get room.
