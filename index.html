Neutrals

  
Neutral and Greyscale are the only sets with separate Light and Dark values. Neutral is the step
  ladder with a hue-75 tint that fades into the shadows on light, and a hue-190 tint that grows into them
  on dark — one expression each.









  
Neutral playground

  
One pair per theme, tint hue free. Green is the perceptual lift — how much lighter a tinted step
  reads than a grey of the same L, in OKLab points. It stays near 2 at any hue, against roughly 24 across the
  chromatic ramps, which is why the neutrals need no lightness correction.
