Continuous curves with a saturation gain per palette

v4 keeps pure functions of t — no step tables, no flat S plateaus — and lifts the light end of L so early samples stay brighter. Each colour family multiplies the saturation drop by its own gain kH, which pulls Mindaro, Pulse, Pink and the rest closer to Figma.

Theme-dependent families

  
Neutral and Greyscale are the only sets with separate Light and Dark values. Neutral is the step
  ladder with a hue-75 tint that fades into the shadows on light, and a hue-190 tint that grows into them
  on dark — one expression each.
