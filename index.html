v5 · full 0–100 domain · sine saturation

Full 0–100 scale with a sine-shaped saturation curve

v5 runs lightness and saturation across the whole 0→100 domain. Named tokens (25, 800, …) are interior samples — not the ends of the gradient. Saturation follows a raised-cosine S-curve: flat at both ends, continuous through the middle, scaled per family by kH.

Theme-dependent families

  
Neutral and Greyscale are the only sets with separate Light and Dark values. Neutral is the step
  ladder with a hue-75 tint that fades into the shadows on light, and a hue-190 tint that grows into them
  on dark — one expression each.
