Reverse-engineered from the Figma variable set

Every ramp from continuous H, S and L

v2 replaces the discrete step ladders with continuous functions of a single parameter t. Named steps sit on an even lightness ladder (96, 90, 85, 75…15) inverted through L(t), so early stops stay light and consecutive tokens feel evenly paced. Figma remains the fit target.

Theme-dependent families

  
Neutral and Greyscale are the only sets with separate Light and Dark values. Neutral is the step
  ladder with a hue-75 tint that fades into the shadows on light, and a hue-190 tint that grows into them
  on dark — one expression each.
