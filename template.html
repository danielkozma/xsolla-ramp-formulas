Neutrals

  
Neutral and Greyscale are the only sets with separate Light and Dark values. Neutral is the step
  ladder with a hue-75 tint that fades into the shadows on light, and a hue-190 tint that grows into them
  on dark — one expression each.









  
Neutral playground

  
One pair per theme, tint hue free. Both ride the plain power law — only S differs — and the diagrams are why
  they carry no lightness correction: green is the perceptual lift, how much lighter a tinted step reads
  than a pure grey of the same nominal L, in OKLab points. It runs about 1.5 on light and 2.2 on dark at the
  shipped tints, and never clears 6 at any hue, against roughly 24 across the chromatic ramps — and it peaks
  mid-ramp rather than at the light end, where the bend does its work. Nothing there to cancel.
